Not connected Playground

Nobody else is here yet. Send the Invite link to another device, or open it in a second browser, and each of you will see the other.

How it works

The channel carries only who is here and what they have switched on. Camera and screen go straight between browsers over WebRTC — the platform never sees a frame.

Every pair of people negotiates its own stream. That suits a handful of people; past six or so, each person's upload runs out and a room wants the platform's SFU instead.

Sharing your screen replaces your camera for everyone rather than adding a second stream. The source is call.js, under 300 lines.
