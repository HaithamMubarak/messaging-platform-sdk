# Running a SponsorPulse event

For the person operating the first few events by hand. It assumes nothing about
the software beyond being able to open a page.

## The day before

1. Open the host console and connect to a room. Pick a room name you will
   recognise later; the password is generated for you.
2. Fill in **Branding**: brand name, event name, date, accent colour, logo.
   The accent is what attendees see behind every button, so use the one the
   room is decorated in.
3. Choose the **attendee language**. This is carried in the join link, so
   whichever you pick is what someone sees when they scan — they can still
   change it themselves. Arabic and Hebrew lay the page out right to left.
4. Fill in **Sponsor**, but leave *Show the sponsor panel* unticked. Reveal it
   during the event rather than having it on screen from the start.
5. Copy the join link or screenshot the QR. Send it to whoever is printing
   table cards or building slides.

**Check before you leave:** open the join link on your own phone, on mobile
data rather than the venue wifi, and make sure you land in the room.

## Thirty minutes before

1. Open the host console and connect to **the same room name and password**.
   Nothing carries over otherwise — this is the one thing worth getting right.
2. Put the QR on a slide, big. A QR on a phone screen held up at the front of
   a room does not scan from row six.
3. Have one segment ready to launch so the first thing people see when they
   join is something to do, not an empty screen.

## Running it

- **One segment at a time.** Launching a new one replaces what is on every
  attendee's screen; scores and the question board carry across.
- **Timers.** A poll with a timer closes itself. Zero means it stays open until
  you end it. Thirty seconds is right for a quiz question; a poll can be longer.
- **Reveal** shows the tally to the room and, for a quiz, awards the points.
  Nothing is scored until you reveal.
- **Q&A is moderated.** Questions land in the console and are shown to nobody
  until you approve one. Pin the one being answered on stage — only one thing
  is pinned at a time.
- **The sponsor panel** appears the moment you tick the box. Good moments: the
  break, and just before the last quiz round.

**If the room gets loud and questions flood in**, hide rather than delete:
hidden questions still count in the report.

## Afterwards

1. **Event summary** gives you the headline numbers and the top five.
2. **Export leads (CSV)** downloads the attendees who ticked the consent box.
   Nobody else is in that file, including people who typed an email and then
   unticked. That is the promise made on the join screen, so do not work around
   it — if a sponsor wants more names, the answer is to ask more clearly next
   time, not to export more.
3. Tell the sponsor the attendee count and the consented count separately. They
   are different numbers and conflating them is how a follow-up email lands
   with someone who did not agree to it.

## What to watch for

- **An attendee says nothing is happening.** They are almost certainly in a
  different room — check the link they used has the same `#r=` as yours.
- **Everyone is stuck on "waiting".** Nothing is launched, or you reconnected
  into a different room than the one on the slide.
- **A name that looks like a formula** (`=SUM…`) is defused in the export; it
  will show with a leading apostrophe in a spreadsheet, which is intended.
- **The room is bigger than you planned for.** The cap is 500 attendees. Past
  that, joins are refused rather than the tally quietly going wrong.

## What this does not do yet

- No billing, and no accounts for anyone.
- Nothing survives the organiser closing the console — the console is the
  authority, so an event is a session rather than a saved document. Export
  before you close the tab.
- No recording, no video.
