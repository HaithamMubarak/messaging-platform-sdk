    Waiting

    Pick a trick, then press Verify.

    Verification runs in your browser. It never asks the platform whether the platform is honest.

    How does it know?

    genesis = evidence-chain:<log> 

    chain₀ = SHA-256( genesis   | contentHash₀ | canonical(stamp₀) )
chainₙ = SHA-256( chainₙ₋₁ | contentHashₙ | canonical(stampₙ) )

    Each note goes into channel storage, and only its hash goes to the platform's attest(). The platform gives it its place in the order, stamps its own clock, welds it to the record before it and signs the result. It never sees the note.

    Verify asks two questions. Does the signed chain still re-derive, checked against the published key? And does every note still hash to what was sealed for it? Edit a note and the second question fails. Delete or swap one and the log no longer lines up with its receipts.

    Your tampering only changes this tab’s copy: storage and the chain are append-only, so there is nothing there to rewrite. That is the realistic threat anyway: somebody hands you a log and says it is the record. Verification is what tells you it is not.
