The full apparatus of justice, aimed at forty pence.

Somebody is in the dock. Anyone may submit testimony anonymously — which is what makes people eloquent — and the bench decides what is admitted. The jury votes in sealed booths.

The verdict and the sentence go onto a record that is appended to, never overwritten. Later trials cite it. That is the retention mechanism and the joke at the same time.
