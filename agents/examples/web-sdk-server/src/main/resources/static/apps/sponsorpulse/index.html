Playground · SponsorPulse

A room full of phones, all answering the same question.

Attendees scan a code, type a name, and they are in — no account, no app, no password. The organiser runs polls, quiz rounds and a moderated Q&A from one screen, and the sponsor gets a panel that appears when it suits the run of show.

In the room

  • Join by QR. The room and its key travel in the link, in the hash rather than the query, so they stay out of Referer headers and server logs.
  • Polls, quiz rounds and Q&A. One segment at a time, with a timer and a live leaderboard when it is a quiz.
  • Answers sized for a thumb. Nothing an attendee taps is smaller than 44px, because they are standing up holding a phone.
  • English, Arabic and Hebrew, right-to-left included — chosen by the organiser and carried in the join link.

The console

  • Moderation before anything is shown. Questions arrive in a queue; nothing reaches the room until it is approved. Pin one, hide one, mark one answered.
  • Your branding and the sponsor's. Logo, colour and event name on every attendee's screen; the sponsor panel is shown when you switch it on, not before.
  • An export you can hand over. A CSV of the attendees who ticked the consent box, and nobody else.

Who decides

One authority, always. Every answer, vote and upvote is addressed to the organiser's console, checked there, and broadcast back as a whole state. Attendee pages render what they are told and never compute a score — which matters, because those pages run on other people's phones and can be edited there.

So the rules live where the tally is: one answer per person, a closed segment stays closed, an option nobody offered is refused, and a per-attendee rate limit stops a script flooding the count. State travels over the channel relay rather than a peer mesh, because a room of a hundred people is not a mesh.

Consent

An attendee's name is shown to the room — that is what a leaderboard is. Contact details are a separate question, asked once, with the box unticked. The export contains only the people who ticked it, and the email field does not even appear until they do.
